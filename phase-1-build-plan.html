The one goal

Definition of done

A seller can create a real listing through the wizard; an admin reviews and publishes it; it appears on the live site & map; and every form submission lands in your inbox as a trackable lead. No more browser-only confirmations.

Everything not needed to hit that goal — search engine, WhatsApp, payments, title workflow — is deliberately deferred to Phase 2. We ship the spine first.

The sprint, week by week

Each week ends on a working, demoable milestone.

Week 1
Foundation
Setup

Database, auth & storage standing up

Create the Supabase project — enable PostGIS, set regions, secrets.
Build the schema — all core tables with row-level security.
Auth — email + phone OTP sign-in, user roles (buyer / seller / agent / admin).
Storage buckets — public for photos, private for documents.
Wire the existing auth.html to real sign-in / sign-up.
Milestone: you can create an account and log in for real.
Week 2
Listings live
Core

The wizard writes real properties

Listings API — create, read, update, delete with ownership rules.
Photo & document upload from list.html straight to storage.
Save the drawn pin, Google Plus Code & all details to the database.
Search & Property pages read from the database instead of the demo array.
Status lifecycle: draft → pending review → active.
Milestone: a property listed through the wizard appears on the live site.
Week 3
Forms & leads
Capture

Every form becomes a lead + an email

Connect email (Resend/Postmark) with a verified sending domain.
Wire valuation, viewing request, message agent, area alerts → leads.
Team notification email on every new lead & new listing.
Auto-reply to the user (valuation estimate, viewing confirmation).
Favorites & saved searches persist to the account.
Milestone: submitting any form really emails your team & the user.
Week 4
Admin & launch
Ship

Review, approve, go live

Admin board — review & approve/reject listings, view documents, see all leads.
Seller dashboard.html shows real status, views & leads.
Security pass — RLS review, consent capture, rate limiting, spam guard.
Deploy to your domain, configure email DNS, lock the MapTiler key.
Smoke test & soft launch with a handful of real listings.
Milestone: MyKunda is live and generating leads.

Week-1 schema (what gets built first)

The minimum tables to hit the Phase 1 goal.

TablePhase 1 scope
usersAuth, role, name, email, phone, consent
listingsFull property/land record + lat/lng + plus_code + status
listing_mediaPhotos (public) & documents (private)
leadsAll form submissions + pipeline stage
viewingsRequests & proposed slots

Deferred to Phase 2: payments, title_verifications workflow, messages real-time threads, and the Meilisearch index. The tables can exist now; the features come later.
3

To start — your checklist

Five things unblock Week 1. Most take minutes.

  • Lead email address — where team notifications & enquiries should arrive.
  • Supabase account — sign up (free); add me, or share project keys securely.
  • Email sender — a Resend or Postmark account, plus the domain you'll send from.
  • Domain — confirm the final domain so auth, links & email DNS are set up once.
  • Admin contacts — who reviews listings & documents (names + emails).
Not blocking Week 1: payment-provider choice (Wave / QMoney / cards) and the WhatsApp Business number — those are needed for Phase 2.
