The vibe

Kololi is the lively heart of The Gambia’s coast: long golden beaches, seafood grills and beach bars, with some of the country’s best-known residential addresses set back from the sand. It sits directly beside Senegambia and the two names are often used interchangeably — but they behave as different markets. Senegambia is the tourist strip and trades on nightly lets; Kololi proper is where the villas, gated compounds and long-term rentals sit, on quieter roads a few minutes from the noise. Prices here are among the highest in the country, and demand comes from diaspora buyers and expatriates who want the beach without living on top of the bars.

Kololi is bought for yield as often as for living, and the two lead to different properties. Read renting out property in The Gambia and the best areas to buy on the coast before you narrow it down.

Kololi's land values are inflated by tourist-strip demand — hotels, restaurants and Airbnb operators compete for plots alongside residents. Construction is standard but finishing costs run high as buyers expect Western-grade kitchens, AC and security.
