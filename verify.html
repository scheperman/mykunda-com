Buying a listing that already carries a Verified badge? You can still order this. The badge tells you the seller passed our listing check; the report is your own copy of the evidence, in your name, covering the things a listing check does not reach — the registry, the boundaries and the people on the ground.

What we actually check

Every report answers the same questions in plain language, and says clearly where the answer could not be confirmed.

There is no licensed agent register — yet

Estate agency is not a regulated profession in The Gambia. There is no licence to ask for and no register to check a name against. A draft Real Estate Agents Bill would introduce mandatory registration and licensing, but it is still a draft and is not in force. Until it is, an independent check on the title is the only protection that does not depend on trusting the person selling. What the bill proposes, and where it stands →

      You are paying for the investigation, not for a particular outcome. A check that uncovers a problem has done its job, and is not refundable on that basis. If we are unable to carry out the check at all — for example because the registry has no record, or the documents you were shown do not exist — we refund what you paid, less any registry or legal costs already incurred on your behalf. We tell you what those costs are before we incur them. Everything is charged in dalasi; amounts in other currencies are indicative.

      How it works

      You do about five minutes of work. We do the rest and come back to you in writing.

      What this is not

      We would rather be useful than impressive. An Ownership Verification is a professional check of what the documents and the registry say on the day we look. It is deliberately not:

      • Legal advice, or a substitute for your own lawyer at completion
      • A guarantee that a title is clean or that a sale will complete
      • A valuation or a survey — we flag when you need either
      • Insurance against a dispute arising later
