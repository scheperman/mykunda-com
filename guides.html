Everything you need to know before you buy

Clear, honest guides to buying, renting and investing in The Gambia — written by our professional team for diaspora, expats and locals. No jargon, just what actually matters.

Thirteen guides that answer, in order, the questions people actually ask before they buy in The Gambia — written from what goes wrong as often as from what goes right.

Buying property here is not difficult, but it is unfamiliar. The obstacles are rarely the price. They are the form of ownership, which is not the same across the country; the paperwork, which is thinner than most buyers expect; the payment, which usually crosses a border; and the build, which almost always takes longer than the seller says.

If you are starting from nothing, read them in this order. Begin with buying property as a foreigner, which sets out what a non-Gambian can and cannot own. Then freehold, leasehold and customary land explained — the single most important distinction on this site, and the one most buyers discover too late. With that in place, how to verify a land title shows you what to check before any money moves.

Once you know what you are buying, the money follows. The real cost of buying covers the fees and duties that sit on top of the asking price. If you need finance, compare bank mortgages against developer financing — in practice they suit very different buyers. And if you are paying from abroad, sending money to The Gambia explains what each route actually costs.

After the purchase come the decisions that last. Building a house walks through permits, costs and supervising a site you may not be standing on. Renting out property covers yields, tenants and tax. Residency and retiring is for anyone planning to spend more than a holiday here. And inheritance and wills for Gambian property deals with what happens to the compound afterwards — the question diaspora families ask us most and plan for least.

Looking for a place rather than a process? The best areas to buy on the Gambian coast compares the coastal areas against each other, and every area in The Gambia lists all 41 with their average price per m².
