Land and property prices in The Gambia

What land actually costs, area by area, in dalasi — the currency it is sold in. Land and buildings are listed separately, because in The Gambia they are usually sold separately, and every figure says how much evidence sits behind it.

Land in the 46 areas MyKunda tracks runs from D13,333 per m² in Fajara down to D71 per m² in Gambissara — a factor of 188 across one small country. The standard unit is a 20 × 20 metre plot of 400 m². An area only gets a rate of its own once we have at least five priced local plot listings with a known size: 12 of these areas clear that bar, and the other 34 carry the rate of the surrounding band or region, marked as such — a single listing never sets an area figure on this site. Upcountry we publish land only — there is not enough house or rental advertising to put a number on either. All of it is young data, honestly labelled: reliable Gambian market data barely exists yet, and building it — listing by listing, figure by figure — is part of why MyKunda exists. For bare plots specifically, see land for sale in The Gambia.

What makes up the price per m²

A square metre on the coast is not expensive because building is expensive — construction costs vary far less across the country than land does. Four things move the number, and their share shifts as you go south and inland:

Land value
Largest share
Position, and above all distance to the beach. This is the component that collapses as you move inland.
Construction & materials
Fairly flat
Cement, blocks, steel and labour cost roughly the same in Brusubi as in Bakau. On cheap land it becomes the dominant share.
Tourist & expat demand
Coast only
Short-let yield potential. Real in Kololi, Senegambia and Cape Point; close to zero upcountry.
Amenities & infrastructure
Smallest share
Tarred access, water, power, schools. Small in money, decisive in whether a plot is buildable at all.

Every area guide shows this breakdown in figures for that specific area — see Kololi for the coastal pattern and Busumbala for the inland one.

How to read these numbers

They are asking prices, not sale prices

Gambia has no public land registry of transaction prices, so nobody — including us — can publish verified sale prices. What we can publish is what sellers and agents ask, consistently measured across all 46 areas. Expect to negotiate; how much depends entirely on the seller and on how long the property has been on the market.

Price per m² is a comparison tool, not a valuation

Two plots in the same street can differ by half on title alone: a registered lease with a clean chain is worth substantially more than customary land with a receipt and a promise. Use the figure to compare areas and to sanity-check an asking price — not to value a specific property. For that you need the papers, which is what Ownership Verification is for.

Dollars, dalasi and your own currency

The table is in US dollars per square metre so the areas stay comparable over time; the dalasi has moved a great deal in the period this index covers. Use the currency switcher in the header to see listings in EUR, GBP or GMD. Conversions are indicative only.
