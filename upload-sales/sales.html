How the numbers are built. Sold is every payment that reached succeeded, on the date it was paid. Fee is the rate above applied to that amount. Net kept is sold minus fees. A refunded payment leaves the sold total but keeps its fee as a cost, because the provider does not give that back. Payments that failed, expired or were cancelled cost nothing and count nowhere except in their own table. Rates are stored in this browser for now — see the note below.

Two things this page cannot do yet, both waiting on the database: the fee rates live in this browser instead of centrally, so a second computer starts from the defaults; and a refund is all-or-nothing, because there is no field for a partial amount. Both are written up for the server side.
