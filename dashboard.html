Email notifications

What we send you, and what you can turn off.

Email me about new messages
Get an email when a buyer or seller sends you a message. Your conversations keep working either way — new messages are always waiting in Messages.
WhatsApp me about new enquiries
A short WhatsApp message when someone asks about one of your listings, with their number so you can answer straight away. Sent to the number on the listing. You always get the email as well.
New-listing alerts
One email a day at most, only when a new listing matches a search you saved. This is the master switch: it turns the alert on — or off — for every saved search at once. You can still silence one search on its own under Saved searches.

Emails about your viewings, your listings and your payments always come through. They are part of the deal itself, so there is no switch for them.
