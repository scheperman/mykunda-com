Why this is needed

The frontend is done. Nothing is stored or sent yet.

Today every form on the site — valuation, List a property, viewing requests, agent messages, account sign-up, area alerts — validates input and shows a confirmation in the visitor's browser only. The data goes nowhere. A web page on its own cannot save records or send email.

This blueprint adds the missing half: a backend (database + API) that persists everything, and a CRM layer that turns each enquiry into a trackable lead your team can act on — so the promise of "we'll get back to you" is actually kept.

Outcome. Sellers can list and manage real properties · every buyer enquiry lands in your inbox and pipeline · alerts and confirmations are really sent by email and WhatsApp · nothing slips through the cracks.

Why Supabase as the core

It bundles the four things this project needs most — a PostgreSQL database with PostGIS for map search, authentication (email + phone OTP, ideal for Gambia), file storage for photos and land documents, and serverless functions for business logic — behind one managed service with a generous free tier. It runs on the static host you already use, so no servers to maintain. A custom Node/NestJS + Postgres stack remains the alternative if you outgrow it.

5

Notifications

Email for records, WhatsApp for reach — the Gambian default.

To your team

  • New lead / valuation request
  • New listing awaiting review
  • Title document uploaded
  • Payment received

To users

  • Valuation estimate & confirmation
  • Viewing confirmed / new slots proposed
  • New listings matching a saved search
  • Message replies

Channels: transactional email via Resend or Postmark; WhatsApp via the WhatsApp Business API (through Twilio or 360dialog) for confirmations and alerts; optional web push for instant match alerts.

Title verification workflow

The trust feature that matters most in The Gambia.

Double-selling and unclear ownership are the biggest risks in Gambian property. The backend makes verification a real, auditable process — not just a badge.

01
Seller uploads docs
02
Secure storage
03
Legal review
04
Verified badge

Documents land in private, access-controlled storage (never public), an admin or partnered lawyer reviews them, and only on approval does the listing earn its Verified title badge — with a timestamped audit trail of who checked what.
