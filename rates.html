All rates come from the Central Bank of The Gambia daily valuation rates — the official published rate — pulled once a day. CBG quotes the dalasi against all three currencies, so EUR → USD and EUR → GBP are cross rates derived from it (EUR/GMD ÷ USD/GMD), not ECB mid-market rates. Expect them to sit a few percent away from what frankfurter.app or Google shows: CBG's indicative rates carry a bank spread. That is correct behaviour — a price quoted in dollars here matches what a Gambian bank charges, and every currency on the site stays on one source. frankfurter.app (ECB) is only used when the CBG scrape returns the dalasi but not USD/GBP; the tile above says which source is live. A daily move over 3% is rejected and logged in fx_rate_rejects rather than applied, so a bad scrape can never reprice the site. Only override manually if the published rate is clearly wrong.
