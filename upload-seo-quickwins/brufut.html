The vibe

Brufut is where The Gambia's coastal property market is moving fastest. Once a quiet farming village set back from the shore, it now hums with construction: gated estates, modern townhouses and generous building plots line freshly paved roads. It sits between the established tourism belt of Kololi and the wild southern beaches of Sanyang, offering a sweet spot of lower land prices, larger plots and proximity to both. For investors and self-builders, Brufut is the area to watch — prices are climbing steeply but remain well below Kololi and Cape Point.
