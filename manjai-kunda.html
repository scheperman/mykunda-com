The vibe

Manjai Kunda is where a lot of Banjul’s working middle class actually lives: two- and three-bedroom houses behind low walls, small compounds let room by room, shops on every corner. It is close enough to Westfield to walk and close enough to the coast road to reach Kololi in a quarter of an hour, without any of the coastal pricing. Buyers here are usually Gambian families or diaspora landlords buying for rental income rather than holidays — the tenant pool is local, reliable and does not disappear in the rains.

Manjai Kunda is squarely a Gambian middle-class market, bought with salaries and bank finance. See bank mortgages and the real cost of buying.
