Buyer & seller protection

How MyKunda keeps you safe

Property fraud and unclear land ownership are the biggest risks in Gambian real estate. We bring proven, professional standards of due diligence and verification to protect both buyers and sellers — here's how it works.

Order an ownership check Ownership Verification explained

Verified titles

We commission the check; a qualified specialist reviews the title deed and ownership documents and confirms the document type before a listing earns its Verified badge.

Vetted partner agents

We employ no agents. The agencies we work with are established Gambian firms, identity-checked, reviewed by real clients, and accountable to us for the listings they handle. Agents can partner with us here.

Clear process guidance

We advise you to use staged payments through lawyers, never on a handshake. We provide guidance from listing to completion.

How title verification works

When a seller chooses a Verified or Managed plan, we put a structured check in motion before the listing goes live with its badge. We offer the service; the work itself is done by qualified specialists — document reviewers, Gambian lawyers and, where boundaries are in question, surveyors. They are engaged for the job and do not have to be on our payroll to be accountable to us for it.

1

Document review

The seller uploads the title deed and ownership documents. A qualified specialist reviews them and confirms the document type — freehold, leasehold or customary.

2

Ownership confirmation

The same specialist checks that the person listing the property matches the documented owner, or holds clear written authority to sell or let it.

3

The Verified badge

Once checks pass, the listing carries a Verified badge and ranks higher in search — a clear signal to buyers that the basics are sound.

4

Your own due diligence

Verification is a strong first filter, not a guarantee. We always recommend your own lawyer runs a final title search before completion.

Honest about what verification is

Our check builds trust and filters out obvious problems. It is not legal advice or a guarantee of title — your lawyer's final search is always the last word.

Verification you buy for yourself

The Verified badge above is bought by the seller, as part of a listing plan, and covers the documents supplied with that listing. Ownership Verification is a separate product bought by the buyer — and it works on any property in The Gambia, including one you found somewhere else entirely.

Verified badge

Ordered by the seller with their listing plan. Documents reviewed, badge shown on the MyKunda listing, priority placement in search. Only for properties listed here.

Ownership Verification

Ordered by you. Documents, registry search by an independent lawyer and — on the full check — a site visit. A written report addressed to you, for any property in the country.

Checking a property that isn't on MyKunda?

That is exactly what this is for. Tell us where it is and what you have been shown, and we will tell you honestly whether the check is worth running.

Red flags to watch for

Wherever you buy, these are the warning signs that should make you slow down — on MyKunda or anywhere else.

Paying safely

How money should move in a property deal — and how it shouldn't.

Use staged payments

A deposit on signing, the balance on transfer and registration — ideally held and released by a lawyer. Never pay the full amount before the deed is in your name.

Get everything in writing

A written sales agreement with names, price, plot details and payment stages protects both sides. Verbal deals are where disputes start.

Diaspora transfers

For international transfers, keep records of every payment and route funds to a lawyer's or escrow account, not a personal one.

See something suspicious?

If a listing looks fraudulent, duplicated or misleading, tell us. We review every report and remove listings that breach our rules.

Pay by mobile money or card, or by bank transfer. Online payments are processed by Waychit, a Gambian payment aggregator that works through payment partners licensed by the Central Bank of The Gambia. MyKunda never sees your card or wallet details.

Bank transfers clear once the banks release them. MyKunda only collects listing and service fees — we never take deposits, down payments or purchase money. Those belong in your lawyer’s client account — insist on that in writing, because no Gambian rule compels it.
