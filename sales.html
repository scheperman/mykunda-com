Fee rates

One row per method per start date — older payments keep the rate they were charged atSaved

A rate applies from its date onwards until a later one takes over. Setting a rate for a date that already has one overwrites that percentage; it never touches the months before it.

How the numbers are built. Sold is every payment that reached succeeded or refunded, on the date the money arrived. Fee is what the provider kept: the amount actually withheld from the payout once it has been reconciled against a bank statement, and until then an estimate from the rate that applied on the payment date, marked ~. Either way it sits on the full amount — the provider keeps its cut whatever happens afterwards. Given back is a refund, counted on the day the money went back, so a September refund of a June sale lands in September. Net kept is sold, minus fees, minus what went back. Payments that failed, expired or were cancelled cost nothing and count nowhere except in their own table.

Pay by mobile money or card, or by bank transfer. Online payments are processed by Waychit, a Gambian payment aggregator that works through payment partners licensed by the Central Bank of The Gambia. MyKunda never sees your card or wallet details.

Bank transfers clear once the banks release them. MyKunda only collects listing and service fees — we never take deposits, down payments or purchase money. Those belong in your lawyer’s client account — insist on that in writing, because no Gambian rule compels it.
