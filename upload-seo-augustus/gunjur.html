The vibe

Gunjur is the largest village on The Gambia’s Atlantic coast — a sprawling, vibrant community with deep cultural roots. Its beachfront mosque, with green domes overlooking the ocean, is one of West Africa’s most photographed landmarks. The Bolong Fenyo Community Wildlife Reserve protects mangroves, wetlands and a stretch of pristine beach. Gunjur sits between the tourist corridor to the north and the quieter south, giving it both accessibility and authenticity. Property prices are still well below Brufut and Bijilo, attracting investors who want coastal land with genuine community character.

The eco-tourism premium reflects interest from European buyers seeking nature retreats.
