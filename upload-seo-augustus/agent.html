About Awa

With eight years of experience on The Gambia's coast, Awa is one of the most knowledgeable property specialists in the Kombo area. She joined MyKunda as a founding agent because she shares our belief that the market needs more transparency, proper verification and professional service standards.

Awa specialises in beachfront homes and land, and is known for her patience with overseas clients and her insistence on clean, verified titles before any deal moves forward. Working online and on the ground across Kombo South, she facilitates the full process: arranging viewings (in person or by video), providing market insights, connecting you with trusted lawyers and notaries, and coordinating build-management for plots.
