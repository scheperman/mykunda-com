How buying works

From first viewing to keys in hand

Buying in The Gambia is straightforward when the paperwork is done properly. This is the route we walk every buyer through.

1

Shortlist & view

Save the listings you like and request viewings. Abroad? We record walkthrough videos and join you on a call from the property.

2

Check the title

Before any money moves, the title deed and ownership documents are reviewed and the document type confirmed — freehold, leasehold or customary.

3

Agree the price

You negotiate directly with the owner or their agent. We keep the numbers and conditions transparent on both sides.

4

Survey & contract

A licensed surveyor confirms the boundaries, and a local solicitor drafts the sale agreement and handles the transfer.

5

Transfer & register

Payment, stamp duty and registration at the Lands Office. You receive the registered documents in your name.
