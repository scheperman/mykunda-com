MyKunda uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. This page lists exactly what we set and why.

1 What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Some are essential for the site to function; others help us improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

3 Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser at any time. Note that blocking essential cookies may stop parts of MyKunda — such as staying signed in or keeping your chosen currency — from working properly.

Good to know: MyKunda stores some preferences (like your currency, favourites and sign-in) directly in your browser so the site works smoothly. These never leave your device unless you send a message or publish a listing.
