Last updated 15 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data MyKunda collects, why, and what rights you have. We aim to collect only what we need and to keep it safe. It applies to everyone who uses mykunda.com — buyers, renters, sellers, landlords and agents.

1 Data we collect

  • Account data — your name, email and phone number;
  • Listing data — property details, photos and documents you upload;
  • Messages — enquiries and chats you send through MyKunda;
  • Usage data — pages viewed, searches and saved favourites, to improve the service.

2 How we use it

We use your data to run your account, publish your listings, connect you with buyers or sellers, verify titles where requested, process payments, prevent fraud, and send you service updates and (if you opt in) area alerts.

3 Sharing

We share your contact details with another user only when you choose to respond to them. We use trusted providers for hosting, payments and email, who process data on our behalf. We never sell your personal data.

4 Your choices & rights

You can access, correct or delete your account data at any time from your dashboard, unsubscribe from marketing emails, and ask us to remove your listings. To request a copy or deletion of your data, email info@mykunda.com.

5 Retention & security

We keep your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. We protect it with encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Your rights under Gambian law: the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act 2025 gives you the right to know what we hold about you, to have mistakes corrected, and to ask for your data to be deleted. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Questions? Email info@mykunda.com. See also our legal overview.
