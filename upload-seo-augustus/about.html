MyKunda is new on the Gambian property market — but the experience behind it isn't. We bring years of proven real estate and technology expertise to build the transparent, professional platform the Smiling Coast deserves.

Our story

MyKunda was born from a simple observation: The Gambia's property market has enormous potential — a young, growing population, a stunning coastline, and a diaspora eager to invest back home — but the market lacks one critical thing: trust infrastructure. Unclear titles, opaque pricing, and no central place to list or find property have held it back for years.

We saw this gap and decided to bridge it. Coming from markets where transparent property transactions, verified ownership and professional service are the norm — we set out to build the platform The Gambia's market needs. MyKunda combines proven real estate technology and processes with deep local knowledge of the Kombo coast and beyond. Whether you're a Gambian abroad looking to build on family land, an expat seeking a sea-view apartment, or a local seller who wants fair exposure — MyKunda is your platform.

We're new here, and we know that trust is earned, not claimed. That's why everything we do — from title verification to flat-fee pricing to honest market data — is designed to prove our value through actions, not words.

What "Kunda" means

In Mandinka, kunda means home, compound, dwelling place. You hear it in every Gambian town name — Serrekunda, Latrikunda, Manjai Kunda. It's the most natural word for where you belong. MyKunda is your place. Your home. Your investment. Your start.
