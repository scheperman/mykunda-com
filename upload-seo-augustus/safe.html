How title verification works

When a seller chooses a Verified or Managed plan, we put a structured check in motion before the listing goes live with its badge. We offer the service; the work itself is done by qualified specialists — document reviewers, Gambian lawyers and, where boundaries are in question, surveyors. They are engaged for the job and do not have to be on our payroll to be accountable to us for it.

1

Document review

The seller uploads the title deed and ownership documents. A qualified specialist reviews them and confirms the document type — freehold, leasehold or customary.

2

Ownership confirmation

The same specialist checks that the person listing the property matches the documented owner, or holds clear written authority to sell or let it.

3

The Verified badge

Once checks pass, the listing carries a Verified badge and ranks higher in search — a clear signal to buyers that the basics are sound.

4

Your own due diligence

Verification is a strong first filter, not a guarantee. We always recommend your own lawyer runs a final title search before completion.

Honest about what verification is

Our check builds trust and filters out obvious problems. It is not legal advice or a guarantee of title — your lawyer's final search is always the last word.

Red flags to watch for

Wherever you buy, these are the warning signs that should make you slow down — on MyKunda or anywhere else.

Paying safely

How money should move in a property deal — and how it shouldn't.

Use staged payments

A deposit on signing, the balance on transfer and registration — ideally held and released by a lawyer. Never pay the full amount before the deed is in your name.

Get everything in writing

A written sales agreement with names, price, plot details and payment stages protects both sides. Verbal deals are where disputes start.

Diaspora transfers

For international transfers, keep records of every payment and route funds to a lawyer's or escrow account, not a personal one.

See something suspicious?

If a listing looks fraudulent, duplicated or misleading, tell us. We review every report and remove listings that breach our rules.
