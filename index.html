Built with Gambian estate agents

The people who know your street sell it best

MyKunda is a platform, not an agency — we employ no agents of our own and we never compete with the professionals on the ground. Instead we are building a network of established Gambian estate agencies: independent firms with years of completed sales here, a reputation in the Kombos and clients who can vouch for them. They keep their own business; we bring the buyers, the reach into the diaspora and the title checks.

Pay by mobile money or card, or by bank transfer. Online payments are processed by Waychit, a licensed Gambian payment provider. MyKunda never sees your card or wallet details.

Bank transfers clear once the banks release them. MyKunda only collects listing and service fees — we never take deposits, down payments or purchase money. Those are always paid into the escrow account of your lawyer or notary.
