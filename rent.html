Two ways to rent

Staying a season or settling in?

The Gambian rental market splits cleanly in two, and the terms are genuinely different. Pick the one that matches your plans.

Short stay

Weeks to a few months, furnished and ready. Common along the Senegambia strip and Kotu during the November–April season.

      How renting works

      From enquiry to moving in

      Renting here is quick, but the deposit and utility arrangements catch people out. Here's what to expect.

      1

      Enquire

      Message the landlord or agent straight from the listing — in-app or on WhatsApp. Say when you want to move and for how long.

      2

      View it

      See the place in person if you can. From abroad, ask for a video walkthrough — check water pressure, power and the compound wall.

      3

      Agree terms

      Settle the rent, the term and what's included. Most landlords ask for one to three months up front plus a deposit.

      4

      Sign & pay

      Get a written tenancy agreement naming both parties, the rent, the term and the notice period. Keep proof of every payment.

      5

      Move in

      Photograph the condition on day one, note the NAWEC meter readings, and confirm who handles repairs and the water bill.
