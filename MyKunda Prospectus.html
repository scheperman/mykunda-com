Executive Summary

Bridging the trust gap in Gambian real estate

The Gambia's property market is valued at over $26 billion and growing at 6.7% annually — yet it has no centralised, trustworthy digital platform for buyers, sellers, or investors. Title fraud, opaque pricing, and fragmented listings prevent billions of dollars in diaspora capital from flowing into real estate.

MyKunda is the first digital property platform for The Gambia. We combine proven real estate technology and verification processes with deep local knowledge of the Kombo coast. Our platform offers verified title deeds, transparent pricing, professional photography, and integrated digital payments via Modem Pay — the country's first licensed fintech gateway.

We are seeking strategic partnerships with Gambian banks, financial institutions, and investors to scale the platform, launch mortgage products, and capture the enormous opportunity at the intersection of a $776M diaspora remittance economy and a booming property market.

Young, growing population

2.8 million people with a median age under 20. Population growing at 2.2% annually — one of the highest in West Africa. 65% urbanised, driving massive housing demand.

Diaspora investment appetite

$776M in remittances — largely used for private construction, land acquisition and family upkeep. The CBG itself recommends better channels for diaspora real estate investment.

Government-backed land reform

The National Land Policy 2026–2035 will digitise land records, create a National Land Administration System, and reduce title verification times — aligned exactly with MyKunda's mission.

6% GDP growth trajectory

Gambia's GDP projected to grow 5.5–6% annually through 2028, supported by the $96M OIC road infrastructure project and increased foreign direct investment.

The Platform

What is MyKunda?

MyKunda is a full-service digital property platform for The Gambia. We don't just list properties — we verify, professionalise and transact them. Our core promise: every listing is real, every title is checked, every price is transparent.

1

Verified listings

Every property gets a title deed check, GPS coordinates (Google Plus Code), professional photos, and accurate pricing based on market data.

2

Digital transactions

Buyers pay deposits and listing fees via Wave, QMoney, Africell, Orange Money, or Visa/Mastercard — processed by Modem Pay (CBG-licensed).

3

End-to-end service

From search to signing: managed viewings, legal coordination, escrow via Trust Bank, and post-sale support. WhatsApp-native for diaspora buyers.

Why MyKunda wins

There is currently no comparable platform operating in The Gambia. Traditional agents operate via word-of-mouth and Facebook groups. MyKunda is the first to bring institutional-grade processes to this market.

Technology

Built for Gambian infrastructure

MyKunda is designed from the ground up for the realities of West African internet: lightweight pages, offline-capable, WhatsApp-native communication, and mobile-first responsive design. Our technology stack:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL) — Scalable database with real-time sync and row-level security
  • Modem Pay SDK — Custom integration for Wave, QMoney, Africell Money, Orange Money + Visa/Mastercard
  • WhatsApp Business API — Transaction confirmations, viewing bookings, agent communication
  • Google Plus Codes — Precise location for every listing, even where street addresses don't exist
  • Server-side rendering — Fast load times on 3G/4G Gambian networks
  • Multi-currency — Prices in USD, GMD, EUR, GBP with live conversion rates
