The vibe

Mansa Konko is the quiet twin of Soma: the government town on the ridge, while the trade and the traffic happen at the junction below. Colonial-era bungalows, regional offices and wide plots make it feel unlike anywhere else upcountry. Buying here means buying into a very small market — a handful of transactions a year, mostly houses rather than land — but the trans-Gambia highway and the new bridge have put both banks within reach, and that is slowly pulling attention this way.

Administrative land and family land sit side by side on this ridge, and they are not the same thing to buy. See freehold, leasehold and customary land explained and how to verify a land title.
