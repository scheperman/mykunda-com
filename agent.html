We are building this network now — carefully

MyKunda works with Gambian estate agents, not against them. We are a platform: we bring buyers, reach into the diaspora and independent title checks — the agent keeps the client relationship and the commission. The agencies that will appear on this page are independent Gambian firms with a track record we can check: years of completed sales, real clients we can call, and a reputation here that predates us. We would rather launch with a short list of agencies we trust than a long list we cannot vouch for — so until the first profiles appear here, every enquiry on MyKunda is handled by our own team, and title checks are carried out by independent qualified specialists as always.

What "partner agent" will mean

Buying or selling in the meantime?

You do not need to wait. List your property yourself for free, add a title check for a Verified badge, or talk to us — in English, at a time that works across timezones.

Are you an estate agent?

If you have been selling property in The Gambia for years and hold yourself to high standards, we would like to hear from you.

Pay by mobile money or card, or by bank transfer. Online payments are processed by Waychit, a licensed Gambian payment provider. MyKunda never sees your card or wallet details.

Bank transfers clear once the banks release them. MyKunda only collects listing and service fees — we never take deposits, down payments or purchase money. Those are always paid into the escrow account of your lawyer or notary.
