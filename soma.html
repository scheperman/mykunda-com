The vibe

Soma is The Gambia's unofficial crossroads — a bustling market town of around 7,000 people where the main east–west highway intersects the Trans-Gambia Highway that links northern and southern Senegal. This strategic position makes it the commercial capital of the Lower River Region and a constant hive of transit activity: bush taxis, trucks loaded with groundnuts, traders heading to Senegal, and travellers stopping for the town's famous dibitair (grilled meat). Soma is not a tourist destination — it's a working town with a vibrant market, a hospital, several schools and a growing diaspora-funded development scene. For property buyers, it offers some of the cheapest land in The Gambia with genuine upside as infrastructure improves along the Trans-Gambia corridor.

Land in Soma is very affordable despite its strategic position. Construction materials are trucked from Greater Banjul (~180 km), adding a moderate transport premium. The town's position on the Trans-Gambia Highway is expected to boost land values as the new Senegambia Bridge increases traffic flow.
