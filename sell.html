        The three bands, in full

        Checking a single plot document takes less work than checking a compound with three, so a smaller property costs less. There are exactly three bands, they are set by your own asking price, and this table is the whole of it — the price you see here is the price you pay.

        Boost is — for every property, whatever it is worth. Amounts in other currencies are indicative — the fee is charged in dalasi.

        How you can pay

        Pay however suits you — a wallet on your phone here in The Gambia, or a card from wherever you are. Every fee is charged in dalasi and confirmed in writing.

        Mobile money

        Wave, Afrimoney, QMoney and APS Wallet. You approve the payment on your own phone and your listing activates as soon as the payment is confirmed. Best for everyday amounts.

        Card

        Visa and Mastercard with 3-D Secure, including cards issued abroad. The obvious choice if you are selling from the UK, US, Europe or elsewhere in the diaspora.

        Bank transfer

        Straight into our GT Bank account with a reference code we generate for you. Sensible for larger amounts. Sending from abroad? Our SWIFT code is on the checkout page.

        Payments are handled through Waychit, a licensed Gambian payment provider. We charge listing and service fees only — MyKunda never collects a deposit, down payment or the purchase price. That money belongs in your lawyer's or notary's escrow account, never in ours.
