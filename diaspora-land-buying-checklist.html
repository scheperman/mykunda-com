Most land disputes in The Gambia are not sophisticated fraud. They are ordinary sales where nobody asked to see the paper trail — and by the time someone does, two families are standing on the same plot. Distance is not what puts you at risk. Running every step of the purchase through a single person is what puts you at risk.

Independent, always

Never the seller, never their agent, never the person who introduced you to the deal.

A flat fee

Never a percentage of the price you are paying — on the checklist or on the paid check.

In writing

A report addressed to you, that you can forward to your lawyer or keep for your own record.

Pay by mobile money or card, or by bank transfer. Online payments are processed by Waychit, a Gambian payment aggregator that works through payment partners licensed by the Central Bank of The Gambia. MyKunda never sees your card or wallet details.

Bank transfers clear once the banks release them. MyKunda only collects listing and service fees — we never take deposits, down payments or purchase money. Those belong in your lawyer’s client account — insist on that in writing, because no Gambian rule compels it.
