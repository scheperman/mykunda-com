The vibe

Gunjur is the largest village on The Gambia’s Atlantic coast — a sprawling, vibrant community with deep cultural roots. Its beachfront mosque, with green domes overlooking the ocean, is one of West Africa’s most photographed landmarks. The Bolong Fenyo Community Wildlife Reserve protects mangroves, wetlands and a stretch of pristine beach. Gunjur sits between the tourist corridor to the north and the quieter south, giving it both accessibility and authenticity. Property prices are still well below Brufut and Bijilo, attracting investors who want coastal land with genuine community character.

Gunjur is a village with village land tenure, however modern the plot looks on a map. Read how to verify a land title and building a house.

These are asking prices actually advertised in Gunjur, not a national average pushed onto the map.

Every figure here is an asking price. The Gambia has no public register of sale prices, so what sellers ask is the only thing that can be measured consistently; actual sale prices are generally lower. We have no rent listing we can verify here, so we quote no rental yield for this area either — a yield built on a rent we had to guess would only look like knowledge.

Reliable Gambian market data barely exists yet — building it is part of why MyKunda exists. We print the evidence behind every figure and leave a blank rather than defend a number we cannot. Know one to be wrong? Tell us.
